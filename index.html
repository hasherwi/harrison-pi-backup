harrison-pi is a personal utility designed to copy files selected by its owner from Google Drive to owner-controlled storage.

What the app does

The utility runs on the owner’s computer. It uses rclone to read selected files and their metadata, then creates backup copies and records needed to check or restore those copies. It is intended for the owner’s personal use.

Access
Read-only Google Drive
Purpose
Personal backup and recovery
Storage
Owner-controlled devices

Permission and control

The requested permission, drive.readonly, allows viewing and downloading files across the authorized Google Drive account. The utility’s local configuration selects the folders to process; the Google permission itself is not limited to those folders.

This permission does not allow the utility to upload, edit, rename or delete files in Google Drive. The owner reviews the Google consent screen and can revoke access through their Google Account.

This website provides app information. It has no sign-in or upload form, does not start a backup, and does not receive Google Drive files or OAuth credentials.
